Our story

Built on Listening, Guided by Experience.

For more than 30 years, Dr. Bobby Thomas has practised dentistry with one question at the centre of every consultation: how can this patient keep their natural teeth healthy for longer?

Today the centre welcomes patients at two clinics in Bengaluru, EGL, Domlur and Magrath Road opposite Garuda Mall. Experienced doctors and modern technology, including laser dentistry and digital dental scanning, support care that is personalised, clearly explained and comfortable.

Our Philosophy

Our philosophy is simple: preserve what is healthy, treat what needs attention and protect the results through appropriate follow-up care.
