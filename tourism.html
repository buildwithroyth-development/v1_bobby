Your Dental Treatment, Planned Around Your Journey.

Travelling for dental treatment requires careful planning, clear communication and an understanding of the procedures involved.

At Dr. Bobby Thomas Dental & Implant Centre, patients visiting Bengaluru from other cities or countries can enquire about consultation and treatment planning before their visit.

Where appropriate, patients may share relevant dental records and X-rays for an initial discussion with the clinic.
